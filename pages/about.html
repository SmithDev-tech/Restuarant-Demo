Who We Are

A Place Where Good Food & Good Times Come Together

Founded in 2016, Yummy City Restaurant was born from a simple but powerful belief: everyone deserves a meal that feels like a celebration. What started as a 12-seat neighbourhood bistro has grown into a beloved dining destination — but our soul has never changed.

We are passionate about creating unforgettable dining experiences. Our chefs use only the freshest, locally-sourced ingredients to craft dishes that honour tradition while embracing modern creativity.

Whether you're here for a quick lunch, a romantic dinner, or a milestone celebration, we promise to make every moment memorable.

Our Journey

A Decade of Milestones

2016

The First Flame

Yummy City Restaurant opened its doors with 12 seats, a wood-fired oven, and an unshakeable vision to bring the neighbourhood together over exceptional food.

2018

First Culinary Award

Recognised by the City Dining Guide as "Best New Restaurant," validating our commitment to quality and creativity in every dish.

2020

Expanding Our Reach

Doubled our seating capacity and launched a weekend brunch menu, welcoming an even wider community of food lovers.

2023

Rooftop Terrace Opens

Added a breathtaking open-air terrace, becoming the go-to sunset dining spot in Yummy City.

2026

A Decade of Flavour

Celebrating 10+ years — still cooking with the same passion, still committed to the same standard of excellence.
